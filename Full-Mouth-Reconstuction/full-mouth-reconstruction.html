A coordinated treatment plan to reconstruct your beautiful smile

If you’re experiencing multiple dental issues that need attention to get your smile back to full health, then a full mouth reconstruction could be a good option for you.

When you come to your appointment, we’ll work with you and answer all of your questions to help you decide if a full mouth reconstruction is right for you.

What is a full mouth reconstruction?

While some patients might just need a treatment or two to fix their smile, patients with multiple issues or a mouth full of decayed or missing teeth might need a little extra TLC.

With full mouth reconstruction, we'll examine your smile to develop a full understanding of the state of your oral health. From there, we'll work with you to develop a coordinated treatment plan that fixes all of the dental problems in a cohesive way. This will ensure that every treatment complements one another and your smile is put in the best position to succeed in the future.

who is a good
candidate for full mouth
reconstruction?

At Sana Samin Dental Care, we often recommend full mouth rehabilitation for patients experiencing the following:

  • Multiple missing or severely decayed teeth
  • Patients looking to replace their dentures with implant treatment
  • A physical trauma that has damaged most of the teeth in the mouth
  • Severe wear caused by bruxism

better smiles through advanced technology

Thanks to our CBCT 3D imaging technology, Dr. Nauman can precisely visualize any health conditions you’re experiencing and develop a personalized full mouth reconstruction for you. This advanced technology provides efficient care, better experiences, and long-lasting results.

what to expect

At Sana Samin Dental Care, we’re dedicated to providing the very best dental care experience.

  • We work with you to carefully outline every step of your treatment so you feel confident in the process
  • Using 3D imaging technology, we’ll visualize the state of your oral health and work with you to develop a treatment plan that works for you
