teeth whitening

What causes stained teeth?

  • Coffee and tea
  • Darkly pigmented foods
  • Smoking and tobacco use
  • The natural aging process
  • Your genetics
→

Who can get teeth whitening?

Almost anyone can benefit from professional teeth whitening, although results vary from person to person. It is generally not recommended for children under 16 years old, pregnant or breastfeeding women, or individuals with allergies to whitening agents.

→

Is teeth whitening good for sensitive teeth?

If you have sensitive teeth, consult your dentist before whitening. Professional whitening treatments are designed to reduce discomfort while providing safe and effective results.
