Are you missing a tooth or suffering from a severely damaged or decayed tooth? At Jackson Family Dental, we offer same-day implant treatment. For patients who just recently experienced tooth loss or damage and have not yet lost any bone structure, same-day implants are a great option.

Don't let damaged or missing teeth affect your long-term oral health or keep you from feeling confident.

what are same-
day
implants
?

Same-day implants and traditional implants are actually the same implant — just with a different process.

The traditional implant process requires healing time prior to placing a crown or other natural-looking restoration. With our best-in-Liberty same-day implant process, we are able to place a temporary, fully-functional crown the same-day as your implant treatment. This temporary restoration will stay in place as your permanent crown is crafted.

who is a good candidate for
same-day implants?

There are lots of scenarios that we recommend same-day implant treatment, including if you...

  • Have a healthy or strong jaw bone
  • Want fast results and a more convenient experience
  • Do not have an underlying medical issue preventing bone from healing properly

The treatment is best for patients who have not experienced any bone loss as a result of their missing or damaged tooth.

Our modern implant technology ensures a more comfortable experience, superior results, and a higher treatment success rate.

The Guided Implant Technique is a sophisticated approach that elevates the dental implant process. This technique involves meticulous planning and utilization of advanced imaging to guide the precise placement of dental implants. This guarantees flawless placement, significantly reduces discomfort, and accelerates recovery time.

By integrating advanced robotics with computerized 3D imaging, the Yomi Implant Robot precisely guides the placement of dental implants, enhancing accuracy and optimizing the overall success of implant surgery.

Combined, these innovative technologies ensure a level of precision that is challenging to achieve through traditional methods.

Are same-day implants safe?

While same-day implants are not the best option for all patients, we only recommend same-day implants to patients who are a good candidate for the treatment.

Am I a candidate for same-day
implants?

Same-day implants are not a good option for patients who have experienced significant decay or resorption. For these patients, a traditional implant process might be necessary. Request an appointment today and find out if same-day implants are right for you!
