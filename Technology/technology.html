our dental technology

At Sana Samin Dental Care, our investment in cutting-edge dental technology is a testament to our dedication to your oral health and overall well-being. Experience the difference of modern dentistry with us, and let us help you achieve and maintain a healthy, beautiful smile.

Guided Implant Technique

Our Guided Implant Technique takes dental implants to the next level. By using advanced software and 3D imaging, we can create a personalized treatment plan tailored to your unique needs. This precision not only ensures the successful placement of implants but also minimizes discomfort and recovery time.

Solea Laser

The Solea Laser is a dental game-changer. This innovative tool allows us to perform various treatments with minimal or no anesthesia. It’s virtually painless and significantly reduces the time needed for recovery. From cavity fillings to soft tissue procedures, the Solea Laser makes your visit to the dentist a breeze.

CEREC Same-Day Crowns

With CEREC Same Day Crowns, we can create and place custom, natural-looking crowns in a single appointment. No more waiting for weeks with temporary crowns – you can leave our office with a permanent restoration. This not only saves you time but also ensures a perfect fit and a beautifully restored smile.

CBCT 3D imaging

Our Cone Beam Computed Tomography (CBCT) 3D imaging technology provides us with an incredibly detailed view of your oral anatomy. This allows for more accurate diagnosis and treatment planning for a wide range of dental procedures, from implant placement to orthodontics. With CBCT, you can trust that your dental care is based on the most comprehensive and up-to-date information.
