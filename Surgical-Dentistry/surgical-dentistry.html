We're proud to provide all-encompassing surgical dental treatments right here in our Liberty office. We can perform nearly every case that comes to us, including wisdom teeth removal, root canals, and even very complex cases. Plus, we specialize in high-tech dental implants.

Our goal is to help you receive all your dental care from the team you know and trust, without the need for an oral surgeon referral. If an outside specialist is the best and safest solution, we partner with a network of amazing oral surgeons and endodontists to ensure you receive five-star care and incredible results.
